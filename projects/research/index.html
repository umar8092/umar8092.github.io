Squig · AI agent · Built on Squig

Multi-source research agent

Pulls signals from several sources at once to build a complete picture of a company: what it runs on, what its customers complain about, and who makes the buying decisions.

How it works

  1. Input: Lead or company (from the CRM)
  2. Research in parallel: Tech stack (scraped from the company site), Web search (targeted queries), Google Maps reviews (customer pain points)
  3. Analyse: AI agent (pain-point analysis and decision-maker identification)
  4. Deliver: CRM (enriched profile upserted)

What I built

  • Scrapes the company's tech stack
  • Runs targeted web searches about the company
  • Pulls Google Maps listings and analyses reviews for recurring pain points
  • Identifies decision-makers, then upserts the full profile back to the CRM

Stack

  • Tech stack scraping
  • Web search
  • Google Maps
  • AI
  • CRM
