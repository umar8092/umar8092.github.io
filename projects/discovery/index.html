Net-new lead discovery agent

Replaces hours of manual prospecting: it searches for new people and companies, then reads their websites to decide whether they're actually showing buying signals.

How it works

  1. Search: Apollo (people and organisation search)
  2. Collect: Candidate companies (and their websites)
  3. Evaluate: AI + web search (scans sites for buying-signal keywords)
  4. Deliver: New prospects (only those with real signals)

What I built

  • Apollo-driven people and organisation search
  • AI and web-search evaluation of each candidate's website
  • Filters on buying-signal keywords so only relevant prospects come through
  • Replaced a manual prospecting process
