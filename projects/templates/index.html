Email template export

Users of an email-template design product can export their HTML templates directly into the email service they already use, through an SDK embedded inside that product.

How it works

  1. Design: Email template editor (user finishes an HTML template)
  2. Export: Embedded SDK (pick a provider, connect the account)
  3. Email providers: Gmail, SendGrid, Mailjet, Zoho Mail, BirdSend and more
  4. Result: Template in the provider (ready to send)

What I built

  • Export flows that push HTML templates into Gmail, SendGrid, Mailjet, Zoho Mail, BirdSend and other providers
  • Delivered through Integry's embeddable SDK, integrated into the customer's product
  • Authentication and account connection handled inside the embedded experience

Stack

  • Embedded SDK
  • Gmail
  • SendGrid
  • Mailjet
  • Zoho Mail
  • BirdSend
