Lead ads to webinar registration

When someone fills in a LinkedIn or Meta lead ad, they're registered for the webinar right away, with no CSV exports and no delay.

How it works

  1. Lead ads: LinkedIn Lead Gen Form, Meta Lead Ads
  2. Capture: Real-time capture (each submission as it arrives)
  3. Map: Field mapping (form answers to attendee fields)
  4. Register: Webinar platform (lead added as attendee)

What I built

  • Real-time capture of LinkedIn Lead Gen Form and Meta Lead Ads submissions
  • Mapping of form fields to the webinar platform's attendee fields
  • Automatic attendee registration in the customer's webinar platform
