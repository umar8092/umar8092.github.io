Multi-directional accounting sync

A two-way sync that keeps a vertical SaaS product and three accounting systems in exact agreement. A change on any side shows up on every other side, without duplicates or overwrites.

How it works

  1. Vertical SaaS: Customer's product (source of operational data)
  2. Sync layer (ID mapping · idempotent upserts · conflict-safe updates)
  3. Accounting systems: MYOB, Xero, QuickBooks

Records kept in sync: Contacts, Invoices, Taxes, Items.

What I built

  • Two-way sync of contacts, invoices, taxes and items across all four systems
  • Cross-system ID mapping so each record is matched to its counterpart everywhere
  • Idempotent upserts, so retries never create duplicates
  • Conflict-safe update logic that keeps data in state parity

Stack

  • MYOB
  • Xero
  • QuickBooks
  • Two-way sync
  • Data integrity
